SHO Course Planner

About this schedule
  • Based on the NIOSH SHO course curriculum, Rev. YS260526 (2026). 22 days.
  • Runs Mon 2 Nov to Tue 8 Dec 2026, on the dates and with the lecturers in additional_info.csv. There is no class 9–16 Nov.
  • Day 7: the PDF puts the afternoon tea break at 15:00–15:15, inside Safe Guarding of Machinery (14:00–16:00). It is shown at 16:00–16:15, with Safe Machine Operation at 16:15–18:15, to match the 2-hour sessions and 8-hour day.
  • Some mornings end at 12:45 with lunch at 13:00, and lunch on Days 5, 10 and 16 runs 13:00–14:30. These are shown as the PDF lists them.
  • Hours of class are worked out from the session times. In a few places these differ from the hour column in the PDF.
  • Remarks are saved in the browser on this device only. They don't sync to other phones or computers.
